# KAH Knowledge Profile v1

Use six core types: `concept`, `claim`, `procedure`, `decision`, `policy`, and `reference`.

Required sections are respectively `definition`; `claim`; `goal` and `steps`; `context` and `decision`; `rule` and `scope`; and `overview`.

Knowledge body language must be `zh-CN` or `en`. Cite every declared source in body Markdown using `[^source-id]`. Cite returned knowledge by its `kah://knowledge/{uuid}?revision={n}#{section-id}` URI.
